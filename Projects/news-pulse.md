## 🧭 Overview & Mission
A self-updating technology-news digest that runs forever on $0 of infrastructure. NewsPulse pulls the day's top tech stories from public RSS/Atom feeds, builds a fast static site, notifies search engines, commits the fresh data, and deploys to GitHub Pages — automatically, every two hours, with no servers, no paid services, and no secrets to manage. **Live site:** https://abhishekanandsupreme-rgb.github.io/news-pulse/ --- [`.github/workflows/update.yml`](.github/workflows/update.yml) is the heartbeat.
